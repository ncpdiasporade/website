Seven death sentences, five asset-confiscation orders: a new compensation precedent in the July trials

The International Crimes Tribunal has sentenced seven figures, including Obaidul Quader, to death for crimes against humanity during the July Uprising and ordered half the assets of five to be confiscated. The next test is enforcement: bringing the fugitives under the law, tracing assets and delivering transparent compensation to martyrs' families and the injured.
